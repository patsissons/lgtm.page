Privacy

lgtm.page and auth.lgtm.page · last updated 2 November 2026

The index (lgtm.page)

The index is a static list of apps. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Your light/dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere.

Sign-in (auth.lgtm.page)

Some apps listed here let you sign in with GitHub or Google through auth.lgtm.page. When you do, auth.lgtm.page receives from the provider you chose: a stable account id, your display name, your avatar URL and, if the provider shares it, your primary email address and whether it is verified. Nothing else is requested; in particular no access to your repositories, contacts, files or mail.

That profile is handed only to the app you were signing in to, as a signed token, so the app can recognise you on later visits. Which apps may use sign-in is limited to the ones listed on lgtm.page.

What is stored

  • A cookie on auth.lgtm.page remembering your sign-in for up to 30 days, so a second app does not ask you to choose a provider again. It is encrypted and holds only the profile above.
  • For about two minutes after each sign-in, a record that the one-time sign-in code was used, so it cannot be replayed.

auth.lgtm.page keeps no database of users, no login history and no provider access tokens. Each app decides what it stores about you under its own policy.

Third parties

Everything runs on Cloudflare. GitHub and Google act as identity providers under their own terms. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Your choices

You can sign out of auth.lgtm.page at any time at auth.lgtm.page/logout, which deletes the sign-in cookie, and revoke the lgtm.page app in your GitHub or Google account settings.
